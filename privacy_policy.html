Privacy Policy

Sukhan — Poetry App

Last Updated: May 4, 2026

Jahan-e-Sukhan ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Sukhan mobile application (the "App"), available on the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Name and email address from your Google account
  • Profile picture URL
  • Google account identifier

We do not access your Google password, contacts, or any other Google account data.

1.2 Usage Data

We collect data about how you interact with the App:

  • Engagement activity — likes, bookmarks, shares, and follows
  • Feed interactions — impressions, views, and time spent on content
  • Search queries — stored locally on your device (not sent to our servers)

1.3 User-Created Content

  • Poetry images you create using the editor
  • Custom background images you upload for poetry creation
  • Bookmark notes you add to saved content
  • Image collections you organize
  • Original poetry you submit if you are an approved Poet (poet mode)

1.4 Device Information

  • Language and theme preferences (stored locally on your device)
We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or advertising IDs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • Personalize your feed with relevant poetry content
  • Save your bookmarks, likes, follows, and collections
  • Generate and store poetry images you create
  • Improve content recommendations based on your engagement
  • Moderate user-submitted content and enforce community guidelines

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device
  • Preferences (language, theme) are stored locally on your device
  • Account data, engagement data, and content are stored on our servers
  • Uploaded images are stored on cloud storage (AWS S3)
  • Firebase Authentication (operated by Google) manages the sign-in process

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Data Shared
Firebase Authentication (Google) User sign-in via Google Email, name, profile photo
Google Sign-In OAuth 2.0 authentication Google account credentials
AWS S3 Cloud image storage Uploaded and generated images
We do not use any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking services.

5. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (Google Firebase and Amazon Web Services) operate data centers in multiple regions, which may include the United States and other countries outside your country of residence. By using the App, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in these jurisdictions. We rely on the standard contractual protections offered by these providers to safeguard international transfers, including for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland.

6. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

Permission Purpose When Requested
Internet Access poetry content and sync data Always required
Photo Library Select background images for poetry editor When you choose to upload an image
Camera Take photos for use as background images When you choose to capture a photo
Photo Library Add Save created poetry images to your device When you save a poetry image

All permissions are optional (except Internet) and requested only when you initiate the relevant action.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Your data may be shared only in these circumstances:

  • With Firebase/Google for authentication purposes
  • With cloud storage providers for image hosting
  • When required by law to comply with legal obligations
  • With your consent when you use the native share feature to share poetry content via other apps on your device

8. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Engagement data (likes, bookmarks, follows) is retained as long as your account is active
  • Generated images are retained until you delete them
  • Local data (search history, preferences) remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App
  • After account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law

9. User-Generated Content & Community Standards

The App allows users to create, upload, and share poetry, custom images, and (for approved Poets) original written work. We have a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive behavior.

To keep the community safe, the App provides:

  • Reporting — you can report any poem, image, comment, or user that violates our community guidelines
  • Blocking — you can block other users to prevent them from interacting with you or your content
  • Moderation — we review reported content and take action (including content removal and account suspension) within 24 hours
  • Poet review — original poetry submitted via poet mode is reviewed before publication

Content that promotes hate, harassment, violence, sexual exploitation, or otherwise violates our community guidelines will be removed and the responsible accounts may be suspended or terminated.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data through the App
  • Delete your bookmarks, collections, and generated images directly in the App
  • Delete your account and all associated data from within the App (Settings → Account → Delete Account)
  • Revoke Google Sign-In access through your Google Account settings
  • Contact us at the email below if you need help with any of the above

10.1 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion of your information, to correct inaccurate information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as defined under the CCPA. To exercise these rights, use the in-app deletion option or contact us at the email below.

10.2 EEA, UK, and Swiss Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. The legal basis for our processing is your consent (sign-in) and our legitimate interest in providing and improving the App. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
